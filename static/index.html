Frequently Asked Questions

Learn what ProofLayer's verification results can and cannot tell you.

Does a risk score prove content is fake?

No. A risk score is an estimate and should be considered alongside other evidence.

What is a SHA-256 fingerprint?

It is a digital fingerprint of content that can help detect whether the underlying data has changed.

Can automated detection be wrong?

Yes. Detection systems can produce false positives and false negatives, so results are not absolute proof of authenticity.

Important: Understand Your Results

ProofLayer provides automated analysis, not a guarantee of authenticity. Detection scores may be inaccurate, and a SHA-256 hash identifies content but does not independently prove who created it or whether it is genuine. Consider additional evidence before making important decisions.
